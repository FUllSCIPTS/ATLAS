# ATLAS Go privacy notice

Effective: 5 November 2026. Applies to ATLAS Go Android version 1.1.0 (`org.atlas.mobility`). Maintainer: FUllSCIPTS / ATLAS. Contact: [ATLAS issue tracker](https://github.com/FUllSCIPTS/ATLAS/issues). Do not put personal information in a public issue.

ATLAS Go is an offline educational model. It does not create accounts, process payments, make real ride bookings or place real food orders. All drivers, menus, routes and delivery stages are illustrative.

Surge outlooks, driver choices and courier timing come from small policies learned offline in a simulated city and bundled with the app. The in-app learning lab retrains a sandbox copy on the device. None of this uses your data, and nothing is sent anywhere.

## Data handled on the device

The app saves simulated ride bookings, selected saved places, shared-seat reservations, cart contents, food orders, activity history and locally generated reference IDs in Android private app storage. Reference IDs use the device time. The app has no Internet or location permissions and no analytics, advertising or third-party collection SDKs. It transmits no app data to the maintainer or another party.

Android backup is disabled for this application. The system still controls device security and app installation. If you contact the maintainer on GitHub, GitHub's own privacy policy applies to that separate interaction.

## Retention and deletion

Local demo data remains until you reset it, clear app storage or uninstall. In the app, open You → Reset all demo data → Reset. There is no online account or server-side data to delete. No data is sold or shared, and no location, contact, camera, microphone, card or identity data is requested.

## Changes

Connecting a backend, payments, maps, analytics or a real transport/food service changes these facts. Update this notice, permissions, consent flows and the Play Data safety form before distributing such a version. This notice describes only the delivered offline release.
